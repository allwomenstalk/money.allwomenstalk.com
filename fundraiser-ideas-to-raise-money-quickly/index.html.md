You can fundraise for almost anything. ## 2. Car Wash

![Car Wash](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/pa/as/a6fzmbpv_589x442.jpg)

Car washes are a great fundraiser during the warmer months of the year. If you have 20 volunteers working at the fundraiser, you can wash several cars at a time and increase the amount of money that you are making. Just make sure that the cars are immaculate in case you want to have another car wash in the future, or pair it with a [good old bake sale](#1) to boost your overall earnings!

## 3. 50/50 Raffle

![50/50 Raffle](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/de/uh/mgztr0ny_600x399.jpg)

The 50/50 raffle is a really great fundraiser for colleges because it gives both the cause and the fundraisers a way to make some money. Have each person donate anywhere from - and divide it into two separate bowls. When each person makes a donation they are given a raffle ticket. When a raffle ticket is picked by the people throwing the fundraiser, the winner gets half of the money that was donated, so everybody wins! It stands out among popular raffle ideas to raise money without significant startup costs.

## 4. Punishment Fundraiser

![Punishment Fundraiser](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/aq/kh/a19ai6xz_600x399.jpg)

These types of fundraisers are also great when trying to attract younger people. The point is that when the cause hits a certain financial goal, one of the people running the fundraiser has to punish themselves in a way; whether it be shaving their head, or being dunked into a pool of ice water. You can manage RSVPs and donations easily through platforms like [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com) to build excitement.

## 5. Dating Auction

![Dating Auction](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/x1/cv/vfv4ttmu_473x338.jpg)

In order to do this fundraiser, you need to know several attractive people who are willing to go on dates with strangers. Basically, a person stands on a stage and is auctioned off to a group of people. Whoever bids the most gets to take the person being auctioned off on a date. They win a date, and the fundraisers get to keep the money for the cause.

## 6. Scavenger Hunt

![Scavenger Hunt](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wo/j1/uio7tz8z_600x399.jpg)

This fundraiser is a really fun way to raise money. It’s quite simple. Everyone who wants to participate in the scavenger hunt must pay a fee in order to start. Whoever wins the scavenger hunt gets a prize. You can also accept online contributions beforehand on crowdfunding platforms like [GoFundMe](https://www.gofundme.com).

Here are a few quick tips for a successful hunt:

- Design 8–10 creative checkpoints across a walkable area.

- Partner with local businesses to sponsor winner prizes.

- Use photo or video proof for team submissions.

## 7. Gift-a-gram

![Gift-a-gram](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/gc/wo/w4im10uk_600x450.jpg)

Depending on the holiday that is closest to the fundraiser, you can have people buy an item that will later be delivered to a person of their choice. For example, if it’s near Valentine’s Day, people can buy a rose for  and the rose will be delivered to their girlfriend or friend. Similar to a [50/50 raffle](#3), gift-a-grams are simple to set up and high margin.
